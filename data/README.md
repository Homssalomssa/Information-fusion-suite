# Data sources and attribution

This repository's local validation datasets include Copernicus Sentinel
imagery and delineation/reference layers from the Copernicus Emergency
Management Service (CEMS) On-Demand Mapping activation EMSN198. Raw data and
outputs are excluded from version control; consult the upstream source records
for product-specific notices and restrictions.

## Copernicus Sentinel data

The official [Sentinel Data Legal Notice](https://sentinels.copernicus.eu/documents/247904/690755/Sentinel_Data_Legal_Notice)
states that Sentinel data are available free, full, and open, subject to
applicable law and its stated limitations and warranty disclaimer. When
communicating or distributing Sentinel data, users must identify the source
with `Copernicus Sentinel data [Year]`. For adapted or modified data, the
notice specifies `Contains modified Copernicus Sentinel data [Year]`.

## CEMS On-Demand Mapping delineations

The current [CEMS On-Demand Mapping Terms and Conditions](https://mapping.emergency.copernicus.eu/terms-and-conditions/)
state that access and use are free subject to the terms and applicable law;
reproduction, distribution, public communication, adaptation, and combination
are permitted within those limits. When communicating or distributing CEMS
On-Demand Mapping data, the terms require informing recipients of the source
using the service's Citation Guidelines. At verification time (2026-11-01), the
terms page linked to `/about/citations-guidelines/`, but that URL returned
HTTP 404. The precise citation format could therefore not be verified; check
the current CEMS citation guidance before redistributing EMSN198 products.
Terms also disclaim warranties, preserve EU ownership, and warn that some
products may be restricted.

This note records the official pages checked on 2026-11-01; it is not legal
advice. Follow any product-specific attribution or access conditions.
